How to use

Enter the frequency of each suit in your ten-card starting hand with the sliders, or type all four digits in sequence. Once the total reaches ten, select Calculate to see the chance that each suit is the Goal suit and the chance that it is the Rare suit.

A suit's chance of being Common equals its same-color partner's chance of being Goal: Spades pairs with Clubs, and Hearts pairs with Diamonds.

How probabilities are calculated

Consider the example hand 5 / 2 / 3 / 0 for Spades, Clubs, Hearts, and Diamonds. The calculator compares that hand with all twelve possible Figgie decks, each containing one 12-card Common suit, two 10-card suits, and one 8-card Rare suit.

For each possible deck, it counts how many ways the same 5 / 2 / 3 / 0 hand could be drawn. Those counts become relative weights and are normalized to total 100%. A suit's Rare chance sums the weights of decks where it has eight cards. Its Goal chance sums the weights of decks where its same-color partner is the 12-card Common suit.
